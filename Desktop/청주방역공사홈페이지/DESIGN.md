## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an authoritative, results-driven commercial presence engineered specifically for ambitious small business owners and executive decision-makers. It balances high-end bespoke craftsmanship with high-converting digital pragmatism. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Tailored Executive Minimalism** infused with **Subtle Luminous Depth**. Deep navy and charcoal midnight canvases project stability, institutional trust, and institutional longevity, while brushed golden brass accents inject energy, conversion-focused hierarchy, and luxury prestige. The emotional impact is confident, unhurried, and distinctly high-value—dispelling the noise of typical agency templates in favor of crisp editorial clarity, precise metrics presentation, and decisive calls to action.

## Colors
The color palette establishes an uncompromising executive hierarchy through strict contrast ratios and luminous focal points:

- **Neutral Foundations (`#0B1320`)**: The deepest midnight base canvas. Surface tiers build upward through midnight slate (`#0F1B2E`) to elevated card surfaces (`#14243B`) and structural containment (`#1A2E4C`).
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37` - Royal Brass)**: Reserved for primary high-conversion drivers, active states, key milestone figures, and structural focal points.
- **Secondary Hue (`#1E2C42` - Slate Navy)**: Deployed for nested card containers, secondary interactive backgrounds, table headers, and inactive button states.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#E5B869` - Warm Aureolin)**: Used for luminous highlights, glowing borders, active hover states, and premium badge fills.
- **Typography & Surfaces**: Text strictly defaults to crisp, unadulterated white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings and high-contrast numerical metrics, shifting to luminous silver-slate (`#CBD5E1`) for running body text and muted bone (`#94A3B8`) for secondary meta labels. Fine structural dividing lines use translucent gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)`).

## Typography
The typographic identity fuses the technological precision and geometric prestige of **Sora** with the ultra-legible, modern cadence of **Manrope**.

- **Headlines (Sora)**: Geometric, confident, and crisp. Tight tracking (`-0.02em`) on display sizes creates a commanding editorial rhythm suited for bold claims, quantitative ROI metrics, and trust-building statements.
- **Body & Captions (Manrope)**: Natural open shapes prevent eye fatigue over technical case studies, value breakdown points, and service deliverables. Open tracking (`+0.01em`) ensures uncompromised legibility on deep midnight canvases.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase styling with intentional letter-spacing (`+0.08em`) on `label-md` and `label-sm` to establish clear structural separation for badges, category chips, and table metadata.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres strictly to an adaptable 12-column grid system optimized for modular blocks, Imweb widget architectures, and cross-device conversion funnels:

- **Desktop (1200px - 1440px max containment)**: Standard 12-column structure with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and fluid outer margins capped at 64px. Elements span discrete multi-column blocks (3-column feature grids, 4-column metric blocks, 8/4 split sales headers).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout. Gutters compress to `1.25rem` (`20px`), margins drop to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Secondary sidebars collapse into sequential vertically stacked cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout collapsing to a single-column linear flow. Outer margin scales to `1rem` (`16px`), gutter to `1rem` (`16px`). Form inputs, CTA buttons, and tier pricing tables take full viewport container width.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Section containers breathe through a strict vertical rhythm: 96px to 120px between core narrative chapters on desktop, scaling down cleanly to 48px to 64px on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Stratification and Warm Brass Backlighting**, avoiding generic cloudy drop-shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Deepest background `#0B1320`. Raw, non-elevated ground.
- **Level 1 (Structural Sections & Baseline Panels)**: `#0F1B2E` with a hairline top border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Form Modules)**: `#14243B` framed by a subtle, precise boundary of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`. Ambient baseline diffusion: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Featured Cards, Active States & Popovers)**: `#182C48` illuminated by an amber halo: `0 16px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`. Border brightens to `1px solid rgba(229, 184, 105, 0.45)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Modals & Primary Menus)**: `#1A2F4D` suspended with `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(2, 6, 15, 0.95)` and an explicit outer hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
The shape language conveys precision, architectural discipline, and structural permanence through controlled, **Soft Geometry (0.25rem / 4px base)**.

- **Micro Components (Chips, Inputs, Buttons)**: Formed with an intentional `4px` corner radius. This avoids both harsh, brutalist sharp corners and overly playful, juvenile high-pill curves, sustaining an exacting B2B executive tone.
- **Macro Components (Panels, Cards, Modals)**: Standardized to `rounded-lg` (`8px`) or `rounded-xl` (`12px`) max, producing refined silhouettes that frame high-density content, client ROI statistics, and conversion funnels with clean discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid brushed gold fill (`background: #D4AF37; color: #0B1320; font-weight: 700; border-radius: 4px; padding: 14px 28px;`). Hover transitions immediately to amber bright (`#E5B869`) with an ambient gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35); transform: translateY(-1px);`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Navy slate fill with gold framing (`background: #14243B; border: 1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35); color: #FFFFFF;`). On hover: border hits `100% #D4AF37` and text takes a warm gold tint.
- **Ghost Action**: Transparent background with white text, underlined by an animated 1px hairline amber bar on interaction.

### Cards & Card Widgets (Imweb Optimized)
- Standardized to `#14243B` surface background with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- Internal padding is set to `space-lg` (`2rem`).
- Highlighted or "Recommended" tiers incorporate a prominent top border rim (`3px solid #D4AF37`) and an internal soft amber radial corner wash.

### Form Widgets & Inputs
- **Base Fields**: Solid `#0B1320` input base, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#64748B`. Height fixed to `48px` with `12px 16px` padding.
- **Focus State**: Hairline transition to `1px solid #D4AF37` with an active inner shadow `0 0 0 1px #D4AF37`.

### Accordion Widget (FAQ & Deliverables)
- **Container**: Enclosed list items with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` separating dividers.
- **Header Trigger**: `#FFFFFF` title font with a dedicated brass chevron indicator (`#D4AF37`). Expands seamlessly to reveal `#CBD5E1` body text inside an elevated `#0F1B2E` inner drawer.

### Chips & Badges
- **Executive Badge**: Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, text `#E5B869`. All-caps styling with `font-size: 11px; letter-spacing: 0.08em; font-weight: 700; padding: 4px 10px; border-radius: 4px;`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px footprint with 3px border-radius. Unchecked state features an inset `#0B1320` core with a slate-gold border. Checked state transitions to a solid `#D4AF37` surface paired with an obsidian checkmark icon.
